02Why deep cleaning matters

Walk them through it

Allergen reduction

Carpet and upholstery hold pollen, dust and pet dander. Hot-water extraction pulls it out of the fibers instead of stirring it back into the air.

Bacteria & germs

Spills, foot traffic and pets feed bacteria in carpet pad, grout lines and cushions. A deep clean flushes what vacuums and mops leave behind.

Dust mites

Mattresses, couches and carpet are where dust mites live. Cleaning on a seasonal schedule keeps them in check between visits.

No residue left behind

Every Coconut van runs reverse-osmosis water. No soap film is left in the fibers, so carpet stays cleaner longer and doesn't re-soil.

Everything in the ducts rides the air, every time the AC kicks on.

  • Dust and pet hair collect on every duct wall and recirculate through each room.
  • Drywall and construction debris often sit in the ducts from the day the house was built.
  • Allergy relief starts at the source. Cleaning carpet without cleaning ducts lets dust settle right back.
  • Dryer vent included. Members get one dryer vent cleaning free every year.
